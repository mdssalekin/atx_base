How we work

From brief to launch, and beyond.

The same five-stage path runs under every engagement, whether it's a five-page site or a full product build.

01

Discover

Week 1

We start with your goals, your users, and the constraints that matter — budget, timeline, and what "done" actually looks like.

02

Design

Week 2–3

Information architecture, UI design, and technical planning, reviewed with you before a single line of production code is written.

03

Build

Sprints

Development in short, visible sprints across web, mobile, or desktop — with staging environments you can check in on any time.

04

Launch

Go-live

QA across devices, performance checks, and a deployment plan that avoids surprises on release day.

05

Grow

Ongoing

Marketing, support, and iteration once real users are in the product — because launch is the start, not the finish.
